# SEO and Answer Engine Optimization

## Define intent and angle
- Identify primary intent: learn compare or buy.
- Choose one ranking angle that matches intent:
  - Buyers guide angle: decision criteria comparison and recommendations.
  - How to angle: step by step process templates and examples.
  - Problem solution angle: pain points root causes and fixes.

## Keyword research workflow
1. Start with a head term and expand into a cluster:
   - Head term: the main topic.
   - Modifiers: best top alternatives vs pricing reviews enterprise open source.
   - Use case terms: sales training onboarding call coaching enablement.
   - Feature terms: AI role play simulation conversation intelligence scoring rubric.
2. Select:
   - 1 primary keyword.
   - 5 to 10 secondary keywords.
   - 10 to 20 supporting terms to sprinkle naturally.

## SERP and competitor analysis checklist
- Skim the top ranking pages and capture:
  - Common section headings and missing sections you can add.
  - What evidence they use and where they are weak.
  - Formats Google rewards for this query:
    - Comparison tables.
    - FAQs.
    - Definitions.
    - Lists with clear criteria.
- Your goal is not to copy structure. Your goal is to be more complete and more usable.

## On page SEO essentials
- Title tag: include primary keyword near the front and add a benefit.
- Meta description: 150 to 160 characters with the outcome and who it is for.
- H1: match the page promise. Avoid stuffing extra keywords.
- Headings:
  - Use H2 for major sections.
  - Use H3 for criteria sub sections and platform reviews.
  - Keep headings descriptive not clever.
- Internal linking:
  - Link to adjacent guides and use cases.
  - Add a short learn more sentence before the link.
- Images:
  - Use diagrams tables and workflow screenshots where allowed.
  - Add descriptive alt text that explains the image content.

## Featured snippet and rich result patterns
- Add a short definition early for What is section.
- Add a 5 to 8 bullet Key takeaways block near the top.
- Use clean tables with consistent columns for comparisons.
- Add an FAQ section with concise answers.

## Answer engine optimization for Perplexity and AI search
Answer engines reward:
- Direct answers early.
- Explicit structure and chunking.
- Verifiable claims that can be traced to sources.
- Short self contained subsections that can be quoted.

Practical tactics:
- Start each major section with a one sentence summary.
- Use clear labels like When to use Pros Cons Best for.
- Keep paragraphs short and avoid buried conclusions.
- Prefer concrete nouns over vague pronouns so answers stand alone.

## EEAT and trust signals
- Include your experience or methodology:
  - How you selected platforms.
  - How you tested them.
  - What data sources you used.
- Add a short author credibility note or methodology box.
- Use third party data where possible and disclose uncertainty when not.

## SEO QA before publishing
- Primary keyword appears in title H1 and early body naturally.
- Secondary keywords appear where relevant without repetition.
- Table of contents works on mobile.
- Every claim that sounds like a fact has a source entry in your source log.
