# Blog Writing Guideline Skill

## Use when
- You need to draft a high ranking blog post or buyer guide for 2025 and beyond.
- You must include research backed facts data stats examples and short case studies.
- You want content that performs in classic search and answer engines like Perplexity.

## Do not use when
- You cannot verify key claims with credible sources.
- The content is sensitive legal medical or financial advice that requires specialist review.
- You are writing short social posts or internal notes instead of a full article.

## Inputs required
- Primary topic and audience.
- Target geography and language.
- Search intent for the page (learn compare buy).
- Primary keyword and 5 to 10 related keywords.
- Competitor URLs or reference articles if available.
- Product or platform list to compare if it is a buyer guide.
- Any first party proof you can share (usage data customer stories screenshots).

## Workflow steps
1. Open SEO_AND_AEO.md and define keyword cluster search intent and the ranking angle.
2. Open BUYERS_GUIDE_PLAYBOOK.md and choose the right article structure and section modules.
3. Open RESEARCH_AND_EDITING.md and collect facts stats quotes and examples with a source log.
4. Write a complete outline with headings that match intent and cover decision criteria.
5. Draft fast using short paragraphs bullets and tables to increase skim value.
6. Add proof blocks throughout (data points mini case studies screenshots where possible).
7. Run the editing checklist for clarity EEAT and answer engine friendliness.
8. Produce final deliverables as Markdown plus a separate sources list for every factual claim.

## Reference map
- SEO_AND_AEO.md: keyword research SERP analysis on page SEO featured snippets and answer engine optimization.
- BUYERS_GUIDE_PLAYBOOK.md: buyer guide structure platform comparison approach review templates and evaluation checklists.
- RESEARCH_AND_EDITING.md: research standards fact checking source logging data use and final editing QA.
