# Templates

Use these as starting structures. Replace bracketed text.

## Cold email to a startup founder
Subject: [Outcome] without [cost]

1. Atomic agreement:
You are building in a world where [constraint] keeps getting harder.

2. Identity validation:
Founders who win here tend to be [identity phrase that matches them].

3. Shared problem:
The cost is that [pain in their language].

4. Frame shift:
A simpler move is [your idea] because it buys [benefit] without [value they care about losing].

5. Proof:
One concrete example:
- [Named example or mini case]

6. Ask:
If I send a 3 line plan for how this would look for [company], should I aim it at you or [role].

## Internal memo

- Next step:
  - [Single ask]

## Landing page hero
- Headline: [Desired future in their words]
- Subhead: [How you deliver it] for [audience] without [pain]
- Proof row: [Metric] [Named customer] [Short claim]
- CTA: [Verb] [Object]

## Narrative pitch
1. World:
In [their world], the rule is [premise they believe].

2. Crack:
But [shared problem] is getting worse.

3. New rule:
So the winning move is [your idea].

4. Mechanism:
It works because:
- [Step 1]
- [Step 2]
- [Step 3]

5. Proof:
[Example] shows [result].

6. Ask:
[Next step].
