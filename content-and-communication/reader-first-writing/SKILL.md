# Reader First Writing Skill

## Use when
- Drafting or rewriting persuasive or explanatory writing.
- Writing cold outreach, internal memos, product narratives, landing pages, or AI prompts.
- You need a repeatable process to match the reader's worldview before introducing a new idea.

## Do not use when
- The task is primarily factual research, data gathering, or citation heavy analysis.
- You must preserve exact phrasing for legal, regulatory, or contractual compliance.
- The user requests purely creative writing with no audience or persuasion goal.

## Inputs required
- Audience or reader persona (who they are).
- The reader’s incentives, worldview, or identity to validate.
- Desired action (what you want the reader to do, believe, or feel).
- Medium and constraints (channel, length, tone, formatting).
- Draft text (optional) and any must-keep points.

## Workflow steps
1. Clarify who the reader is and what they want to be true. Then choose the identity you will validate. (Open `RECEPTION.md`.)
2. List 3 to 7 atomic agreements and 3 to 10 in group signals that fit the reader’s tribe. (Open `FIT-THEIR-WORLD.md`.)
3. Build a frame shift sequence from their world to your idea. (Open `FIT-THEIR-WORLD.md`.)
4. Add world building with concrete examples and micro details so the idea feels real. (Open `WORLD-BUILDING.md`.)
5. Run a cognitive hospitality edit pass to reduce mental load and resistance. (Open `COGNITIVE-HOSPITALITY.md`.)
6. If the output is a prompt, constrain the universe, set rules, and add few shot examples. (Open `AI-PROMPTING.md`.)
7. Deliver the final draft plus a short checklist showing which principles were applied. (Open `TEMPLATES.md` for formats.)
