# Reception First Writing

## Core idea
Most writers think about what they want to say. Strong writers think about what the reader will hear.

## Don’t express yourself
- Avoid writing to perform intelligence, taste, or identity.
- Write to reflect the reader back to themselves.

## Target the reader identity
The opening should validate a self image the reader wants to inhabit.

Examples of identities to validate:
- Startup founder: obsessed builder, speed, leverage, missionary energy, super lean teams.
- Operator: reliability, clarity, risk reduction, predictable execution.
- Creative: originality, taste, signal, community.
- Executive: outcomes, alignment, narrative control, low noise.

## Quick diagnosis questions
Answer these before drafting:
- What does the reader already believe is true.
- What do they want to be seen as.
- What is the status game in this room.
- What makes them skeptical.
- What is the smallest next step they will accept.

## Opening heuristics
- Start with agreement, not novelty.
- Validate effort and constraints the reader faces.
- Show you understand their scoreboard and tradeoffs.

## Common failure modes
- Leading with your story and credentials instead of their stakes.
- Over explaining concepts the reader already has.
- Using language from a different tribe than the reader.
