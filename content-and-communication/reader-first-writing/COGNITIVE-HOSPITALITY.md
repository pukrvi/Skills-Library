# Cognitive Hospitality

## Goal
Make your writing a clean operating system that runs easily in the reader’s brain.

## Reduce the immune response
The brain rejects ideas that feel complex, foreign, or high effort.

Methods
- Use ordinary words.
- Short sentences when stakes are high.
- Familiar metaphors from the reader’s world.
- Declare the structure early so the reader can relax.

## Respect the reader
Simplicity is not dumbing down. It is empathy.

## The simplicity pass
Do this edit pass at the end:
1. Replace abstractions with concrete nouns and verbs.
2. Cut throat clearing and warmup paragraphs.
3. Remove hedges and qualifiers that add no meaning.
4. Split long sentences.
5. Delete duplicate ideas.
6. Make the ask unmistakable and singular.

## Layout rules
- One idea per paragraph.
- Use headings when the topic shifts.
- Prefer bullets for lists of actions, criteria, or options.
- Keep paragraphs under about five lines where possible.
