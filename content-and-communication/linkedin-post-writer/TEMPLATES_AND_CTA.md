# Templates and CTA

## CTAs that do not feel desperate
Use one call to action only and match it to the value.

Best CTA types.
- Comment a word. Best for giveaways.
- Answer a question. Best for discussion.
- Follow for part two. Best for a series.
- DM me. Best for high intent niche.

CTA rules.
- One CTA only.
- Match the value.
- Keep it one sentence.

Fallback CTAs.
- If you want the full checklist comment CHECKLIST.
- Want my template. I will DM it.

## Giveaway operating system
1. Create the lead magnet first.
2. Write the post second.
3. Prepare responses third.
4. Post and respond fast.

Must have assets.
- The PDF doc or checklist.
- A delivery method such as attachment or tracked link.
- Comment and DM templates.
- A simple tracking sheet.

Messaging templates.
- Comment reply. Awesome I will DM you now.
- Connection request. Thanks for the comment. Please accept so I can send you the resource.
- DM. As promised here you go followed by the link or file.

Fallback if you cannot DM everyone. Pin a comment with the link and still ask a question.

## Copy paste post templates

### Template A. Data driven playbook
1. Metric hook.
2. Credibility bullets.
3. Biggest learning.
4. Five to nine bullets of the system.
5. Template or checklist.
6. Benefits.
7. Call to action plus a question if engagement is the goal.

### Template B. Contrarian
1. X is not Y.
2. Why people believe it.
3. Why it fails.
4. What to do instead as a framework.
5. One example.
6. Call to action.

### Template C. Story
1. I started here.
2. Then this happened.
3. I changed.
4. Lesson.
5. Steps.
6. Call to action.

### Template D. Tactical steps
1. Promise.
2. Steps one through seven.
3. Common mistakes.
4. Call to action.
