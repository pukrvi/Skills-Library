# Editing and Publishing

## The 30 minute writing workflow
1. One sentence. I am writing this for who who want outcome but struggle with problem.
2. Choose one structure and do not freestyle.
3. Draft ugly for ten minutes.
4. Tighten the hook for five minutes by rewriting the first two lines five times.
5. Cut thirty percent. Delete fluff and keep the lesson.

Fallback starters.
- I used to think X until Y.
- Most people do X here is why it fails.
- I tracked X for Y days. Surprising result Z.
- If you want X stop doing Y.

## Credibility without bragging
Use the humility formula.
Proof then surprise then lesson then system.

Credibility sources.
- Results metrics.
- Process such as a checklist.
- Receipts such as screenshots.
- Experience.
- Learning from failure.

Never fake specificity.

## Style guide
- Short lines and white space.
- Bullets and minimal bolding.
- Minimal emojis if any.
- Remove filler words.
- Prefer verbs over adjectives.
- Keep jargon only if the audience uses it.

Hashtags. Use a few relevant ones and do not rely on them.

## Editing passes
Run these after drafting.
1. Structure. Each section does its job and transitions into the lesson.
2. Specificity. Replace vague words with numbers timeframes and concrete outcomes.
3. Remove fluff. Cut preambles disclaimers extra examples.
4. Skimmability. Break long paragraphs and turn lists into bullets.
5. CTA alignment. Ensure the call to action is earned by the value.
6. Risk check. No confidential info no unverifiable claims no unapproved client naming.

Fallback rule. Delete thirty percent of words after editing.

## Publishing and engagement
First hour playbook.
- Reply fast.
- Ask follow up questions.
- Deliver promised assets quickly.

Comment reply patterns.
- Validate plus ask a question.
- Clarify plus guide.
- Bridge to DM when relevant.

If engagement is slow.
- Add a pinned comment with a question.
- Repost with a new hook angle.
- Turn it into a short series.

## Repeatable content system
Create three to five content pillars.
Use the one idea to five posts method.
Keep a swipe file of hooks CTAs templates and questions that worked.
