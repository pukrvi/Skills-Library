# LinkedIn Post Writer Skill

## Use when
- You are writing a LinkedIn post and want a repeatable workflow and structure.
- You need stronger hooks, clearer value, and a single aligned call to action.
- You are turning notes, metrics, or a lesson into a skimmable post.

## Do not use when
- You are writing long form articles or newsletters.
- You cannot share the story or proof without breaking confidentiality.
- You need legal or compliance approval language.

## Inputs required
- Primary action for the post: engagement, growth, leads, or authority.
- Primary reader: role, context, pain, and desired outcome.
- Evidence you can use: numbers, story, receipts, process, or learning.
- One main idea: framework, checklist, script, or steps.
- Any asset for a giveaway: link, file, or DM delivery approach.

## Workflow steps
1. Choose the primary action and primary reader.
2. Pick one archetype and one structure before drafting.
3. Draft fast and complete, then rewrite the first two lines five times.
4. Teach one clear idea using a framework, checklist, script, or steps.
5. Highlight benefits and end with one call to action that matches the value.
6. Run the editing passes and risk check before posting.
7. After posting, respond to comments quickly and deliver any promised asset.
