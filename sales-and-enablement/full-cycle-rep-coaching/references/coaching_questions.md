# Coaching Question Patterns

Great coaching questions make the rep re-live a specific moment and discover the insight themselves. These patterns help you generate high-quality questions tied to real transcript evidence.

## Pattern 1: Replay the Moment

Template: "In your call with [Account] on [date], when [buyer] said [specific quote], you [specific action]. What was your thinking in that moment?"

Why it works: Forces the rep to recall their decision-making process. Often they'll recognize the gap themselves.

Example: "In the Demo Magic call on Feb 20, when Chris mentioned concerns about demo reliability, you immediately offered a video library backup. What was your read on his concern at that point?"

## Pattern 2: Alternative Path

Template: "When [specific situation happened], you [did X]. What if instead you had [alternative approach]? How do you think [buyer] would have responded?"

Why it works: Opens up new possibilities without being prescriptive. The rep imagines a different outcome.

Example: "When the buyer said 'budget is tight this quarter,' you moved to discussing pricing tiers. What if you had asked 'What would it cost your team to NOT solve this problem for another quarter?' - how might that have changed the conversation?"

## Pattern 3: Self-Assessment

Template: "If you could go back to your [Account] call, is there one moment you'd handle differently?"

Why it works: Gives the rep ownership. They often identify the same gaps you found.

## Pattern 4: Pattern Recognition

Template: "I noticed in your last [X] calls, [pattern]. Do you notice that too? What do you think drives it?"

Why it works: Helps the rep see trends they can't see from inside a single call.

Example: "Across your last 3 calls, your talk percentage ranged from 62% to 88%. On the DeleteMe call where you talked less, the buyer was much more engaged. What do you think that connection is?"

## Pattern 5: Skill Transfer

Template: "In your [good call] with [Account], you did [specific strength]. How could you bring that same approach to [weaker area]?"

Why it works: Builds on existing strength rather than focusing on deficit.

Example: "In the DeleteMe call, you asked 5 great follow-up questions and the buyer opened up. In the Demo Magic call, you asked fewer questions and did more telling. What was different about your mindset going into each call?"

## Anti-Patterns (avoid these)

- "How do you think your calls are going?" — too vague, gets a vague answer
- "You need to ask more open-ended questions" — this is instruction, not coaching
- "Why didn't you..." — puts the rep on the defensive
- "Do you think you could improve your discovery?" — yes/no question, dead end
