# Follow Up Paths

## Option A Performance coaching
Deep dive prompts
- SPICED layering evaluation by element with missed follow ups.
- Storytelling technique breakdown using situation pain solution outcome.
- Talk to listen ratio pacing and interruption review.
- Objection handling and missed buying signals.
- Emotional intelligence and presence moments.

## Option B Buyer intelligence and deal strategy
Deep dive prompts
- SPICED scoring with follow up questions for weak areas.
- Follow up email outline based on call insights and next steps.
- Risk and obstacle assessment with mitigation actions.
- Persona and buyer type analysis and how to adapt messaging.
- Next call strategy with three to four focused items.
