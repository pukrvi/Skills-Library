# Output Format

Use this exact structure and headings.

## CALL SUMMARY
- Duration
- Buyer
- Seller
- Call type
- Call focus

## NEXT STEP RECOMMENDATIONS
Recommend two to three actions tied to buyer signals.

## RISKS AND RED FLAGS
Use buyer quotes and map to risk type and mitigation.

## BUYERS JOURNEY INSIGHT
- Stage
- Evidence quote for stage
- Next step strategy

## SPICED SUMMARY
One to two sentences on completeness.

## SPICED BUYER QUALIFICATION SCORE
Table with
- Situation score out of 2
- Pain score out of 4
- Impact score out of 4
- Critical event score out of 3
- Economic buyer score out of 2
- Decision score out of 5
- Total score out of 20
Include brief evidence or Insufficient evidence.

## CALL MANAGEMENT PERFORMANCE SCORE
Table with five dimensions each out of 2 and total out of 10.
Include evidence or missing items.

## STRENGTHS
Limit to one to two behaviors linked to buyer response.

## AREAS FOR IMPROVEMENT
Top two to three opportunities
- Missed moment
- What happened with rep quote
- Better approach with exact wording
- Why it matters

## SPICED DETAIL SECTION
For any SPICED element not max
- What was captured
- What was missing
- How to improve with a next time question

## CALL MANAGEMENT DETAIL SECTION
For any skill scored below 2
- What happened
- What was missing
- Example of excellence in short script form

## COMBINED ANALYSIS
- Overall performance out of 30
- Critical insights
- Recommended actions

## WHAT DO YOU WANT TO DO NEXT
Offer two paths
- A performance coaching deep dive
- B buyer intelligence and deal strategy
