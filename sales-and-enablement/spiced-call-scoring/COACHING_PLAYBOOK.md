# Coaching Playbook

## Coaching principles
- Anchor feedback in specific moments and quotes.
- Focus on the smallest behavior change that improves outcomes.
- Provide exact replacement questions and talk tracks.

## How to write strengths
- Name the behavior.
- Show the buyer response.
- Explain why it worked.

## How to write improvements
For each improvement provide
- The missed opportunity
- The rep line that triggered it
- The better line to use next time
- The reason it matters in deal terms

## Question laddering templates
- Situation. Walk me through your current process.
- Pain. What is the biggest challenge with that today.
- Consequence. What does that cause downstream.
- Quantify pain. How often does that happen and what does it cost in time or dollars.
- Emotional stakes. How is that affecting you or the team personally.
- Impact. If this were solved what would be different.
- Quantify impact. What number should improve to call this a win.
- Critical event. Why now and what happens if this slips.
- Decision. Who else is involved and what is the approval path.
- Criteria. What are the must haves and deal breakers.

## Risk based coaching
If critical event is missing coach the rep to find a forcing function.
If economic buyer is missing coach the rep to map authority and budget.
If decision process is missing coach the rep to map steps and timeline.
