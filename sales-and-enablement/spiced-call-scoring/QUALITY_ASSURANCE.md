# Quality Assurance Checklist

## Before you deliver
- Every awarded SPICED point has a buyer quote.
- Any led witness pattern is scored zero.
- Any missing evidence is labeled Insufficient evidence.
- Totals add up correctly and match the component maximums.
- Perfect scores only if all sub criteria are met.
- Call management scores are justified with observable evidence.

## Common over scoring traps
- Treating buyer agreement as evidence.
- Using company facts not stated on the call.
- Converting vague language into numbers.
- Assuming budget authority from title.

## Output hygiene
- Keep tables readable on mobile.
- Keep improvement sections specific with exact replacement lines.
- Keep recommended actions time bound when possible.
