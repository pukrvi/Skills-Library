# Call Management Rubric

## Total 10 points across five dimensions
Each dimension is scored 0 1 or 2.

## 1 Call opening and agenda 2 points
1 point Basic opening
- Some rapport attempt.
- Agenda mentioned.
- Buyer acknowledges.

2 points Professional opening all required
- Genuine rapport with engagement.
- Clear agenda with specific topics.
- Time check confirmation.
- Upfront contract for next steps.
- Buyer explicitly agrees to all.

## 2 Talk to listen ratio 2 points
1 point Balanced
- Buyer talks slightly more than rep.
- Some open questions.
- Basic acknowledgments.

2 points Optimal
- Rep listens sixty percent or more.
- Clarifying questions.
- Paraphrasing summarizing.
- Buyer monologues thirty seconds or more.
- Rep uses silence.

## 3 Storytelling and pitch customization 2 points
1 point Generic story
- Story told but could apply to anyone.
- Weak connection to buyer situation.

2 points Compelling custom story all required
- Similar customer profile.
- Specific pain match.
- Negative consequences.
- Positive rational outcomes with metrics.
- Positive emotional outcomes.
- Resonance check and strong confirmation.

## 4 Confirming value 2 points
1 point Basic check
- Closed question with yes no response.

2 points Deep value confirmation all required
- Open ended value question.
- Buyer specifies what was valuable.
- Why follow up to deepen.

## 5 Call closure 2 points
1 point Attempted close
- Next steps mentioned.
- Some time reserved.

2 points Professional close all required
- Five minutes reserved for closing.
- Value confirmation completed first.
- Specific next step stated clearly.
- Buyer agreement obtained.
- Meeting scheduled on the call.

## Evidence requirements
- Quotes and observed behaviors must support each score.
- If evidence is missing score zero and state what was missing.
