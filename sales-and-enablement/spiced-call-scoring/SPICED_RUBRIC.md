# SPICED Rubric

## S Situation 2 points
1 point Basic context
- Company size industry tools role tenure or similar specifics stated by buyer.

1 point Deeper context
- Team structure reporting line fiscal timing funding KPI ownership or similar specifics stated by buyer.

## P Pain 4 points
1 point Problem stated
- Specific business challenge stated by buyer.

1 point Consequences described
- Buyer explains what the problem causes in process or outcomes.

1 point Rational pain quantified
- Buyer provides a number for loss time rate drop headcount or volume.

1 point Emotional pain expressed
- Buyer expresses strong emotion stakes or personal pressure.

## I Impact 4 points
1 point Benefits acknowledged
- Buyer states the solution would help.

1 point Specific impacts named
- Buyer names concrete outcomes such as rep focus scale outbound catch deals earlier.

1 point Rational impact quantified
- Buyer states a number for improvement savings revenue cycle reduction hours saved.

1 point Emotional impact stated
- Buyer describes human outcome such as morale weekends back board confidence.

## C Critical Event 3 points
1 point Timeline mentioned
- Buyer mentions urgency or a timeframe.

1 point Specific date anchored
- Buyer anchors to a date or event with a clear deadline.

1 point Consequences of missing date
- Buyer explains what happens if the deadline is missed.

## E Economic Buyer 2 points
1 point Decision role identified
- Buyer clarifies their role in decision making and recommendation.

1 point Final authority confirmed
- Buyer states they can approve budget or sign or have final authority.

## D Decision 5 points
1 point Basic criteria
- Buyer lists at least one concrete requirement such as integration budget security.

1 point Specific criteria detailed
- Buyer provides a list of requirements deal breakers or weights.

1 point Process steps mentioned
- Buyer describes steps such as IT review procurement legal pilot.

1 point Process mapped with timeline
- Buyer includes a timeline by step or month.

1 point Champion indicators
- Needs three or more indicators across influence accepts guidance information access mobilization ability.

## Evidence requirements
- Every point must include a buyer quote.
- If the rep leads the witness and buyer only agrees then score zero for that point.
