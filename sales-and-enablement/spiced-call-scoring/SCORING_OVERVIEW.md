# Scoring Overview

## Two part scoring
1. SPICED buyer qualification score
- Total possible 20 points.
- Based only on the buyers explicit statements.

2. Call management performance score
- Total possible 10 points.
- Based on seller execution and buyer response.

## Interpretation bands
SPICED total out of 20
- 0 to 5 Needs major discovery.
- 6 to 10 Partially qualified.
- 11 to 15 Well qualified.
- 16 to 19 Exceptionally qualified.
- 20 Perfect score and extremely rare.

Call management total out of 10
- 0 to 3 Needs significant improvement.
- 4 to 6 Developing skills.
- 7 to 8 Solid execution.
- 9 to 10 Exceptional performance.

## Deliverables produced
- Call summary.
- Next step recommendations.
- Risks and red flags with buyer quotes.
- Buyers journey stage and stage based strategy.
- SPICED scorecard table with evidence.
- Call management scorecard table with evidence.
- Strengths.
- Areas for improvement with better phrasing.
- Combined analysis and recommended actions.
