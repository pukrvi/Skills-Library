# Business Case Scorecard

Score each section from 1 to 5.

## Rubric

| Score | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 5 | Strong, specific, evidenced, and safe to forward. |
| 4 | Good enough to use, with minor gaps or assumptions. |
| 3 | Directionally useful, but needs validation before executive use. |
| 2 | Weak, generic, or assumption-heavy. |
| 1 | Missing or unsupported. |

## Sections

### 1. Pain Specificity

Does the case clearly describe the current workflow, pain, affected teams, and business consequence?

### 2. Evidence Quality

Are claims backed by quotes, notes, metrics, or explicit assumptions?

### 3. Urgency

Does the case explain why the buyer should act now instead of later?

### 4. Decision Logic

Does it compare realistic options and connect the recommendation to buyer criteria?

### 5. Value Quantification

Is the value model transparent, conservative, and tied to buyer-specific inputs?

### 6. Stakeholder Alignment

Does it speak to the champion, economic buyer, users, technical buyer, and blockers?

### 7. Risk Handling

Does it name likely objections and provide credible mitigations?

### 8. Next Step Clarity

Is the requested next action concrete, owned, and time-bound?

## Verdict

| Average | Verdict |
| --- | --- |
| 4.5 to 5.0 | Ready to forward. |
| 3.8 to 4.4 | Usable with minor validation. |
| 3.0 to 3.7 | Useful internally, not yet executive-ready. |
| Below 3.0 | Needs more discovery before use. |
