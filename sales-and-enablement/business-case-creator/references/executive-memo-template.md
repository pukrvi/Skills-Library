# Executive Memo and Champion Pack Templates

## Executive Memo

Use this structure for a buyer-ready decision memo.

### Headline

One sentence that states the business decision and the outcome at stake.

### Executive Summary

Summarize the case in 3 to 5 bullets:

- What is happening now.
- Why it matters.
- Why the current approach is insufficient.
- Recommended path.
- Expected value and next decision.

### Current State

Describe the workflow, affected teams, known pain, and evidence.

### Business Impact

Quantify the cost of inaction where possible. If not possible, show directional impact and the validation needed.

### Options Considered

| Option | Upside | Downside | When it works | Evidence quality |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Status quo | | | | |
| Build internally | | | | |
| Extend existing tools | | | | |
| Buy recommended solution | | | | |

### Recommendation

State the recommended path and why it best fits the decision criteria.

### Success Metrics

Use measurable outcomes:

- Time saved.
- Cost reduced.
- Revenue protected or created.
- Cycle time improved.
- Risk reduced.
- Adoption or quality target.

### Risks and Mitigations

List the risks a skeptical executive would raise and the mitigation for each.

### Next Step

Name the specific decision, meeting, pilot, validation step, procurement action, or executive alignment needed next.

## Champion Email

Subject: Business case for [initiative/outcome]

Hi [stakeholder],

Based on the team discussions so far, I think we should align on [decision]. The short version:

- Current issue: [specific problem and evidence].
- Business impact: [quantified or directional impact].
- Options considered: [status quo/build/extend/buy summary].
- Recommended path: [recommendation].
- Why now: [trigger, deadline, risk, or opportunity].
- Next step: [specific ask].

I included the key assumptions below so we can pressure-test the case before making a final decision.

Thanks,  
[Champion]

## One-Pager

### Decision

What should the organization decide?

### Problem

What is broken today, who is affected, and what evidence proves it?

### Impact

What is the business cost or risk?

### Recommendation

What should change and why?

### Proof

What evidence supports this recommendation?

### Plan

What happens next, who owns it, and when?

### Open Questions

What must be validated before final approval?

## Deck Outline

1. Decision and executive takeaway.
2. Current state and trigger for change.
3. Business impact and cost of inaction.
4. Options considered.
5. Recommended path.
6. Expected value and success metrics.
7. Risks, mitigations, and validation plan.
8. Decision request and next steps.

## Full Report

1. Executive summary.
2. Source material and evidence quality.
3. Current state.
4. Pain and impact analysis.
5. Decision criteria and option comparison.
6. Value model.
7. Recommendation.
8. Stakeholder talking points.
9. Objections and responses.
10. Implementation or next-step plan.
11. Assumption and validation log.
12. Scorecard and gap plan.
