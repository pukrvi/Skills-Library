# Discovery Gap Questions

Use these questions when the evidence is not strong enough to support the business case.

## Current State

- Walk me through the current process from trigger to outcome.
- Who touches this workflow, and where does work slow down?
- What tools or spreadsheets are involved today?
- Which steps are manual, duplicated, or invisible to leadership?

## Pain and Impact

- How often does this issue happen?
- What happens when it is not fixed?
- Who feels the impact most directly?
- What does this cost in time, revenue, risk, or customer experience?
- What work gets delayed because of this?

## Urgency

- Why is this a priority now?
- What changed recently?
- Is there a deadline, renewal, audit, board initiative, or growth target tied to this?
- What happens if the team waits another quarter?

## Decision Criteria

- What would a strong solution need to do to be approved?
- What would disqualify a vendor or approach?
- Which criteria are must-haves versus preferences?
- Who gets final say, and who can block the decision?

## Value Model

- How many people or teams are affected?
- How frequently does this workflow happen?
- How long does the current approach take?
- What is the loaded cost or revenue impact of that time?
- Which metric would prove the project worked?

## Option Comparison

- What would it take to solve this internally?
- What existing tools could be extended?
- What makes the status quo risky or expensive?
- What tradeoffs would leadership accept?

## Implementation and Risk

- What systems must this integrate with?
- What change management would be required?
- What data, security, or compliance requirements matter?
- What has prevented similar projects from working before?

## Champion Enablement

- Who else needs to believe this case?
- What objections do you expect from finance, IT, legal, leadership, or users?
- What proof would make the decision easier?
- What internal meeting or decision should this support?
