# Company and Account Research Framework Skill

## Use when
- You need to produce a investor research style memo on a company for account research.
- You want a consistent structure that blends narrative and analysis.
- You have a target company name and URL and want a repeatable research workflow.

## Do not use when
- You are writing a short sales one pager or outbound snippet.
- You do not have permission to use or share sensitive or confidential information.
- You need legal or financial advice beyond publicly available analysis.

## Inputs required
- Company name and website URL.
- Memo audience and intended use (investor memo, account plan, briefing).
- Word count target (default 1000 to 2000 words).
- Time window for recency (default last 12 to 24 months for traction and news).
- Known constraints (no paywalled sources, no customer names, no speculation).

## Workflow steps
1. Open RESEARCH_SOURCES_AND_NOTES.md and collect the minimum research set.
2. Open MEMO_STRUCTURE.md and create the memo outline with section headers.
3. Draft the thesis and founding story first to establish the narrative arc.
4. Fill product, market, competition, and business model using evidence and clear claims.
5. Add traction and valuation with the best available numbers and comparable context.
6. Write opportunities and risks as paired, specific, and testable statements.
7. Add 2 to 5 exhibits using CHARTS_AND_EXHIBITS.md guidance.
8. Run the editing checklist in STYLE_AND_TONE.md and finalize.

## Reference map
- MEMO_STRUCTURE.md: section by section guidance for the memo.
- RESEARCH_SOURCES_AND_NOTES.md: what to research and how to capture notes and claims.
- STYLE_AND_TONE.md: Investor research style rules, editing passes, and citation handling.
- CHARTS_AND_EXHIBITS.md: exhibit ideas and how to format charts and tables in markdown.
- TEMPLATE.md: copy paste memo skeleton.
