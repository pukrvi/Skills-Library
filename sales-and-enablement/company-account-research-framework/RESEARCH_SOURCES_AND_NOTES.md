# Research Sources and Notes

## Goal
Collect enough evidence to support crisp claims without turning the memo into a link dump. Prefer primary sources when possible.

## Minimum research set
- Company website. product pages. blog. pricing. docs. changelog.
- Leadership and hiring signals. team page. jobs page. roles being hired.
- Funding and ownership. press releases. investor announcements.
- Customer signals. case studies. testimonials. reviews. community posts.
- Market context. analyst reports. industry benchmarks. public filings for incumbents.
- Competitors. positioning pages. pricing pages. product docs.

## Data to capture
- Dates and timelines. founding. launches. funding rounds.
- Quantitative traction. ARR. customers. users. growth rates. retention. NRR. GMV.
- Pricing and packaging. tiers. seat based versus usage based. free plans.
- Ecosystem. integrations. partners. marketplaces.
- Proof points. quotes from executives. customer outcomes. measurable claims.

## Note taking format
Use this format in your scratchpad so you can write fast later.

- Claim:
- Evidence:
- Why it matters:
- Confidence: high. medium. low.
- Source link:

## Handling gaps
- If a number is unavailable. use a range and show your assumptions.
- If you infer. label it explicitly as an inference and explain why it is reasonable.
- Never fabricate customer names. revenue. or valuations.

## Citation guidance
- Use inline citations if the deliverable requests them.
- Prefer footnote style with numbered links at the end of each section.
- If the prompt forbids inline citations but also requests them elsewhere. follow the latest explicit instruction and note the conflict.
