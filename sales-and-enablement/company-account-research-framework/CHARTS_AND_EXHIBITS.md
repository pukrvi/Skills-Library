# Charts and Exhibits

## How many exhibits
Include 2 to 5 exhibits when helpful. Each exhibit should support an argument in the text.

## Good exhibit types
- Market sizing table with assumptions for TAM, SAM, SOM.
- Competitive differentiation matrix.
- Funding and valuation timeline.
- Business model table. pricing tiers and packaging.
- Customer persona map. buyer versus user.
- Product workflow diagram in bullets.

## Markdown formats
### Simple table
Use a markdown table for comps and pricing.

### Lightweight chart alternatives
If you cannot produce a true chart. use:
- A table with ordered values.
- A small ASCII bar like ||||| for relative magnitude.
- A bullet ladder that shows cause to effect.

## Exhibit captions
- Title the exhibit with what it proves.
- Add a one sentence takeaway under the exhibit.
