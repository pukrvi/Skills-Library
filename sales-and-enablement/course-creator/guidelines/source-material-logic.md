# Source material logic

## Supported source material

Course Creator can use:

- Product documents
- Battlecards
- Pitch decks
- Sales methodology docs
- Call transcripts
- Customer stories
- Persona documents
- Process docs
- Existing lessons or assessments
- Uploaded PDFs, documents, slides, or text

## Extraction checklist

When source material is available, extract:

- Target audience
- Product or process concepts
- Required behaviors
- Terms and definitions
- Personas
- Pain points
- Objections
- Proof points
- Examples and stories
- Compliance or policy requirements
- Content that should become lessons
- Content that should become knowledge checks
- Content that should become graded assessment items

## Conflict handling

If sources conflict:

1. State the conflict.
2. Prefer the most recent or most authoritative source if obvious.
3. Ask a targeted clarification if the conflict affects the final course.

## Gaps

If source material lacks examples or scenarios, generate realistic placeholders and label them as assumptions.

## Citations

If the environment supports citations, cite source material in the course pack. Cite especially where product facts, policy details, or customer examples are used.
