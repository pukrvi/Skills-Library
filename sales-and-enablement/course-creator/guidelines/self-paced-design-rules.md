# Self-paced design rules

## Scope

Course Creator builds only self-paced courses. It can create lessons, knowledge checks, assessments, feedback forms, job aids, and optional learner-owned application tasks.

## Allowed patterns

| Pattern | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Read and reflect | Lesson explains a concept and asks the learner to reflect. |
| Watch and answer | Video or embedded content followed by knowledge check. |
| Scenario decision | Learner chooses the best response to a buyer or workplace situation. |
| Written application | Learner writes a response that is manually reviewed. |
| Pitch recording | Learner records a self-paced pitch for manual review. |
| Sorting task | Learner orders steps, priorities, or criteria. |
| Matching task | Learner maps terms, personas, pains, or examples. |
| Flip card | Learner checks recall for key terms or talk tracks. |
| Feedback form | Learner rates course usefulness and clarity after final assessment. |

## Disallowed patterns

Do not create:

- Live workshop agendas
- Facilitator guides
- Instructor notes
- Breakout room tasks
- Group discussion prompts that require synchronous facilitation
- Live role play sessions
- Live coaching sessions
- Classroom timing plans

## Converting live ideas into self-paced equivalents

| Live idea | Self-paced conversion |
| --- | --- |
| Breakout discussion | Reflection prompt or free response question |
| Live role play | Pitch assessment or scenario-based knowledge check |
| Facilitator demo | Short lesson example or video block |
| Peer feedback | Rubric-based self-review or manual reviewer workflow |
| Workshop exercise | Lesson activity plus knowledge check or assessment |
| Manager coaching | Optional post-course adoption suggestion, outside core course |

## Lesson length recommendations

| Course type | Lesson length | Module length |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Microlearning refresher | 3 to 7 minutes | 5 to 15 minutes |
| Standard enablement course | 5 to 12 minutes | 15 to 35 minutes |
| Certification course | 8 to 15 minutes | 25 to 45 minutes |
| Onboarding course | 7 to 15 minutes | 30 to 60 minutes |

## Content density rules

- One primary idea per lesson.
- One practical takeaway per lesson.
- Add a knowledge check after difficult or important lessons.
- Avoid creating long text blocks without examples.
- Use scenario practice when behavior change matters.
- Use a final assessment when the course needs readiness validation.
