# Stage 8 eval: Quality Reviewer

## Quality checks

| Check | Pass condition |
| --- | --- |
| Business alignment | Course maps to business goal and learner behavior |
| Audience fit | Language, difficulty, and examples fit target learners |
| Self-paced integrity | No live training artifacts are included |
| LMS validity | Uses supported LMS entities and question types |
| Assessment validity | Graded items test stated objectives |
| Learner experience | Course is clear, sequenced, and not overloaded |
| Feedback loop | Feedback form and improvement plan are included |
| Measurement | Kirkpatrick-style learning, reaction, behavior, and results measures are considered |

## Quality review summary format

```markdown
| Check | Status | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business alignment | Pass | ... |
```
