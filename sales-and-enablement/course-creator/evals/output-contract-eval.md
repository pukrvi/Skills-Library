# Output contract eval

Use this before final delivery.

## Required final sections

- Course brief
- Design assumptions
- Instructional design choices
- Course settings
- Course map
- Module build plan
- Lesson content plans
- Knowledge checks marked ungraded
- Assessments marked graded
- Final assessment module
- Feedback form
- Reinforcement and retention plan
- Measurement plan
- LMS build blueprint
- Optional tool-call plan
- Quality review summary

## Blocking issues

Block final delivery if:

- The course is not self-paced.
- The final module does not contain a graded assessment.
- There is no feedback form or rating and feedback setting.
- Knowledge checks and assessments are not clearly separated.
- Manual review items lack reviewers or rubrics.
