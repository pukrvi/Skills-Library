# Stage 1 eval: Training Intake Analyst

## Required fields

| Check | Pass condition |
| --- | --- |
| Course topic | Specific enough to design modules |
| Target learner | Role, experience, or team is identified |
| Business goal | Business outcome or operational goal is stated or assumed |
| Learner behavior | Desired behavior change or knowledge outcome is stated |
| Course type | Onboarding, product launch, skill mastery, certification, process, or other |
| Delivery format | Must be self-paced |
| Source material | Present, absent, or requested |
| Constraints | Time, difficulty, audience, or LMS constraints are captured |

## Blocking issues

Ask clarifying questions if:

- The target learner is unknown.
- The course topic is too broad.
- The user requests live training or workshops.
- The business goal and desired learner outcome are both missing.

## Pass output

```markdown
Stage 1 status: Pass
Assumptions:
- [Assumption]
Clarification needed before LMS publishing:
- [Item or none]
```
