# Tell-Show-Do-Apply

## What it is

Tell-Show-Do-Apply is a practical lesson structure:

1. Tell the learner the concept.
2. Show a strong example.
3. Have the learner practice.
4. Ask the learner to apply it in their work.

## How Course Creator uses it

Use this as the default structure for most sales enablement lessons because it is simple and practical.

| Step | Self-paced output |
| --- | --- |
| Tell | Short concept explanation |
| Show | Example call snippet, talk track, email, demo step, or account analysis |
| Do | Knowledge check, sort, match, write, record, or scenario decision |
| Apply | Job aid, account prompt, CRM task, call prep prompt, or reflection |

## Use when

- Building quick product training.
- Teaching a talk track.
- Teaching a process.
- Creating short lessons for busy learners.

## Output example

```markdown
## Lesson using Tell-Show-Do-Apply

Tell: A strong discovery question connects pain to business impact.
Show: Weak question: "What are your challenges?" Strong question: "What happens if this process does not improve this quarter?"
Do: Choose the best follow-up question for a CFO persona.
Apply: Add one impact question to your next meeting prep note.
```
