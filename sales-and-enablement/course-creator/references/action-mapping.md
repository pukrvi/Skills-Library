# Action Mapping

## What it is

Action Mapping starts with a measurable business goal, identifies the actions learners must take to reach that goal, and then designs practice that supports those actions.

## How Course Creator uses it

Use Action Mapping in Stage 2 to prevent content dumps.

## Required mapping

| Item | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Business goal | What business result should improve? |
| Learner action | What must learners do differently? |
| Practice | What realistic activity will build that action? |
| Information | What information is required to perform the action? |
| Metric | How will success be measured? |

## Use when

- The user gives a revenue or operational goal.
- The course should improve behavior, not just awareness.
- Sales enablement wants measurable outcomes.

## Output example

```markdown
## Action map

Business goal: Increase stage 1 to stage 2 conversion.
Required action: Ask impact-oriented discovery questions.
Practice: Diagnose buyer pain and choose the strongest follow-up question.
Information needed: Discovery framework, buyer pain examples, objection examples.
Metric: Assessment score, call review behavior, conversion trend.
```
