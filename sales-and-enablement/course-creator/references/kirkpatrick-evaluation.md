# Kirkpatrick evaluation

## What it is

Kirkpatrick evaluates training at four levels:

1. Reaction
2. Learning
3. Behavior
4. Results

## How Course Creator uses it

Use Kirkpatrick during final quality review and measurement planning.

| Level | Course Creator output |
| --- | --- |
| Reaction | Feedback form questions |
| Learning | Knowledge checks and assessment scores |
| Behavior | Self-paced application task or post-course observation metric |
| Results | Business outcome tied to the original goal |

## Use when

- The user asks how to measure course impact.
- Enablement needs to show business value.
- The course is linked to sales performance.

## Output example

```markdown
## Measurement plan

Reaction: Learner rating and feedback form.
Learning: Final assessment pass rate and question-level analysis.
Behavior: Manager or system review of discovery questions used after completion.
Results: Stage 1 to stage 2 conversion over the next quarter.
```
