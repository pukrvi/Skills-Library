# ADDIE

## What it is

ADDIE is a structured course design process:

1. Analyze
2. Design
3. Develop
4. Implement
5. Evaluate

## How Course Creator uses it

Use ADDIE as the macro operating system for complete course creation.

| ADDIE phase | Course Creator stage | Output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Analyze | Stage 1 | Course brief, learner profile, business goal, constraints |
| Design | Stage 2 and Stage 3 | Behavior map, objectives, course map, assessment placement |
| Develop | Stage 4, Stage 5, Stage 6 | Lessons, knowledge checks, assessments, feedback form |
| Implement | Stage 8 | LMS blueprint or tool-call plan |
| Evaluate | Stage 9 | Quality review and measurement plan |

## Use when

- Building a complete course.
- Creating onboarding or certification.
- Aligning many stakeholders.
- Building an enterprise-ready course pack.

## Do not use as

A reason to overbuild. If the course is urgent, keep ADDIE as the backbone and use SAM for iteration.

## Output example

```markdown
## ADDIE-based course brief

Business goal: Improve enterprise AE discovery quality.
Learners: New enterprise AEs, 0 to 6 months in role.
Performance gap: Reps ask feature-led questions and miss business impact.
Design implication: Build self-paced modules that move from concept to scenario practice to graded final assessment.
Evaluation: Measure assessment score, manager review completion, and stage 1 to stage 2 conversion.
```
