# Microlearning

## What it is

Microlearning breaks learning into short, focused units that support a specific outcome.

## How Course Creator uses it

Use microlearning for short lessons, knowledge checks, recaps, and spaced reinforcement.

## Design rules

- One lesson should focus on one clear idea.
- Keep practice close to the concept.
- Prefer short scenarios over long exposition.
- Use follow-up nudges for retention.
- Use job aids for workflow application.

## Use when

- Learners are busy sales reps or customer-facing teams.
- The course must fit into a short time window.
- The content supports a product launch, process update, or refresher.

## Output example

```markdown
## Microlearning reinforcement

Day 0: Complete lesson and ungraded knowledge check.
Day 3: Answer a single scenario question.
Day 7: Review a one-page job aid.
Day 14: Complete a short final refresher question.
```
