# Kemp Model

## What it is

Kemp is a flexible instructional design model that considers learners, objectives, content, strategies, resources, support, and evaluation as interconnected parts.

## How Course Creator uses it

Use Kemp during intake and architecture to adapt the course to real constraints.

## Questions to apply Kemp

- Who are the learners and how experienced are they?
- What constraints exist for time, format, language, access, and source material?
- What support will learners have after the course?
- What content formats will fit the LMS and learner context?
- What evaluation method is practical?

## Use when

- Learners vary by region, role, tenure, or product line.
- The course must fit a fixed time limit.
- Source material is partial or uneven.
- Accessibility and global readiness matter.

## Output example

```markdown
## Kemp adaptation

Constraint: Learners have only 30 minutes per week.
Design choice: Split the course into 4 short modules with 2 lessons each.
Constraint: Learners are experienced sellers.
Design choice: Skip basic product overview and start with buyer scenarios.
```
