# Example: Stage 2 behavior map

| Business goal | Learner behavior | Current mistake | Practice activity | Metric |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Improve stage 1 to stage 2 conversion | Ask impact-oriented discovery questions | Reps stop at surface pain | Scenario single select and free response | Assessment score and call review signal |
| Improve qualification quality | Identify missing decision criteria | Reps accept vague timelines | Sorting and matching activity | Deal inspection quality |

## Design implication

The course should not be a generic discovery overview. It should use buyer scenarios, weak versus strong examples, and a final assessment that tests judgment.
