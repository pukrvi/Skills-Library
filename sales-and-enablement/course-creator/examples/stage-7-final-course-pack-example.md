# Course Pack: Enterprise Discovery Fundamentals

## 1. Course Brief

Takeaway: This self-paced course helps new enterprise AEs improve discovery quality by practicing impact-oriented follow-up questions.

Target learners: New enterprise AEs
Business goal: Improve stage 1 to stage 2 conversion
Learner behavior goal: Ask follow-up questions that connect pain to business impact
Delivery format: Self-paced only
Estimated duration: 65 minutes

## 2. Instructional Design Choices

- Course process: ADDIE for full course structure.
- Behavior mapping: Action Mapping to tie the course to stage conversion.
- Lesson design: Tell-Show-Do-Apply for practical skill transfer.
- Scenario practice: CCAF and Merrill for realistic buyer decisions.
- Assessment: Bloom and Dick and Carey for graded readiness validation.

## 3. Course Map

| Module | LMS items | Objective | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| 1. Discovery mindset | Lesson, Knowledge Check | Explain why business impact matters | 12 min |
| 2. Impact questions | Lessons, Knowledge Check | Choose stronger follow-up questions | 18 min |
| 3. Deal diagnosis | Lesson, Assessment | Diagnose weak discovery | 20 min |
| 4. Final Assessment and Feedback | Final Assessment, Feedback Form | Demonstrate readiness and provide feedback | 15 min |

## 4. Final Assessment Module

Module title: Final Assessment and Feedback
Items:

1. Assessment: Discovery Readiness Check Graded
2. Feedback Form: Course feedback Last learner-facing step

## 5. Feedback Form

Implementation: Enable Rating and Feedback in course settings. If a separate form entity is available, place it after the final assessment.

Questions:

1. How useful was this course for your role?
2. Which lesson helped most?
3. What remains unclear?
4. How confident are you applying this in your next discovery call?
5. What should we improve?
