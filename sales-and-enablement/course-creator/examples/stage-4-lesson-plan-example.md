# Example: Stage 4 lesson plan

## Lesson: Turn surface pain into business impact

Objective: Given a buyer pain statement, choose a follow-up question that uncovers business impact.
Methodology: Tell-Show-Do-Apply, Merrill
Estimated time: 8 minutes

### Content blocks

1. Rich Text Block: Why surface pain is not enough
   - Key message: Discovery only creates value when it connects pain to business impact.
   - Example: Weak question versus strong question.

2. Rich Text Block: The impact question pattern
   - Explain the pattern: pain, consequence, urgency, owner.

3. Divider

4. Rich Text Block: Example buyer situation
   - Buyer says: "Our reps waste time searching for content."
   - Strong follow-up: "What does that delay cost you during active deal cycles?"

Learner takeaway: Strong discovery questions make the business cost visible.
