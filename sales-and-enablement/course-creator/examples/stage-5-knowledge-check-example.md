# Example: Stage 5 knowledge check

## Knowledge Check: Choose the strongest impact question Ungraded

Purpose: Practice identifying an impact-oriented discovery question.
Question type: Single Select
Framework: CCAF, Merrill
Graded: false

Context: A VP Sales says, "Our reps spend too much time looking for the right content."
Challenge: Choose the best follow-up question.

Options:

A. How many pieces of content do you have?
B. Which content management system do you use today?
C. What happens to active deals when reps cannot find the right content quickly?
D. Do your reps like your current content?

Correct answer: C

Feedback: C connects the pain to business impact. The other options gather information but do not uncover urgency or consequence.
