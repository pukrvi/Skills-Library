# Example: Stage 6 assessment

## Assessment: Discovery Readiness Check Graded

Purpose: Verify the learner can apply impact discovery in realistic buyer situations.
Passing percentage: 80 percent
Retakes: Allowed, max 2
Randomize questions: Yes
Randomize answer options: Yes

### Question 1

Type: Single Select
Auto-graded: Yes
Bloom level: Apply
Question: A buyer says, "This is not a priority this quarter." What is the best next response?
Options:
A. Ask when it will become a priority.
B. Explain why your solution is different.
C. Ask what business initiative is taking priority and what is at risk if this problem remains unresolved.
D. Offer a discount.
Correct answer: C
Feedback: C explores priority, business context, and consequence before pitching.

### Question 2

Type: Free Response
Auto-graded: No
Manual reviewer required: Yes
Reviewer: Manager or named enablement reviewer
Bloom level: Evaluate
Prompt: Write a three-question discovery sequence for a CFO who is concerned about productivity loss.
Rubric:
- Connects to business impact: 0 to 3
- Uses clear and concise language: 0 to 2
- Sequences questions logically: 0 to 3
- Avoids pitching too early: 0 to 2
